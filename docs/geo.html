Patch's whole pitch is "describe a job, get a reasoned shortlist." So the strategic prize isn't a blue link on page one — it's being the answer when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Claude for "the best pizza caterer in Hackney." This is where that stands after this pass.

Why GEO ≠ SEO here. AI crawlers often don't run JavaScript, cite structured facts over prose, resolve the entity before quoting it, and lift answer-shaped pages wholesale. Patch was already strong on the first (everything is server-rendered) and weak on the rest. This pass closed most of the gap.

Foundations — Live

Server-rendered content

Live · was already

Every content page (home, about, for-vendors, profiles, the new service pages) is a server component. AI crawlers that never execute JS still get the full content — the fundamental most JS sites fail.

Entity disambiguation (the "Patch" problem)

Live

Answer-shaped content — Live & Next

Location × category landing pages

Live · the big one
