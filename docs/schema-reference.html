The six enums that constrain status and category columns.

A listing's lifecycle; only 'live' is public.

draft · live · paused · rejected

An enquiry's progress; 'booked' is the outcome signal.

sent · viewed · replied · booked · declined · expired

Mirrors Stripe; drives the vendor's tier.

active · past_due · cancelled · trialing

Account role (renamed from 'parent').

buyer · vendor · admin
